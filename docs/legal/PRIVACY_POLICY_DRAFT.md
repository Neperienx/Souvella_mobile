# Souvella Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-05-19

This is a product draft for review before publication. It is not legal advice.

## Who We Are

Souvella is a private memory-circle app that lets users create or join circles and share memories with circle members.

## Data We Collect

- Account data: email address and authentication identifiers.
- Profile data: default username and optional profile photo.
- Circle data: circles joined, invite codes, circle-specific nicknames, and membership records.
- Memory data: text notes, photo memories, voice notes, captions, dates, likes, and comments.
- Technical data: device/app logs needed for security, debugging, syncing, and abuse prevention.

## Why We Process Data

We process data to:

- create and secure user accounts,
- show memories to members of the correct circle,
- sync memories for offline access,
- support likes, comments, and circle participation,
- provide account deletion and data management,
- protect the service from misuse.

## Legal Bases Under GDPR

Depending on the feature, processing may rely on:

- contract necessity, to provide the app the user requested,
- consent, for optional profile photos, photos, microphone access, and uploaded media,
- legitimate interests, for security, fraud prevention, and service reliability,
- legal obligation, if applicable.

## Offline Storage

Souvella stores synced circle data locally on the user's device using SQLite so memories remain available offline. This local cache can include memories, media payloads, likes, comments, and member nicknames. Removing the app or clearing app data may remove the local cache from that device.

## Sharing

Memories are shared only with members of the relevant circle. Users should only invite people they trust. Souvella does not sell personal data.

## Retention

Data is kept while the account or relevant circle content exists. When an account is deleted, account data and associated personal records should be deleted or anonymized according to the app's deletion behavior and legal requirements.

## User Rights

Users in the EU/EEA may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete their personal data. Users may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from the app profile screen. Before production release, the final deletion behavior must be verified and documented, especially whether memories remain in circles as anonymized content or are deleted.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children unless the final product and store listing explicitly support the required age category and parental consent rules.
